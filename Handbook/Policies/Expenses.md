# Expenses

We pay back what you spend on the studio's behalf, if it was agreed beforehand or is in the table below.

## What you can claim without asking

| Item | Limit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Train travel to a client | Standard class | Book at least a week ahead where you can |
| Lunch with a client | £40 a person | Name the client on the claim |
| Books and type specimens | £60 a month | They join the studio's shelf |
| Software for a project | £25 a month | Cancel it when the project ends |

## How to claim

1. Keep the receipt, a photo is fine.
2. Add it to the month's expenses sheet by the last Friday.
3. You are paid with the next month's salary.

Anything over £250 needs agreement from a director before you spend it.
