# Leave

Everyone has **28 days** of holiday a year, plus public holidays. The studio closes between Christmas and New Year, and those days do not come out of your 28.

## Booking leave

- Book at least **two weeks ahead** for anything longer than two days.
- Check the [client list](../../Clients/Client%20list.md) first: avoid weeks with a launch.
- Put it in the shared calendar once agreed.

## Illness

Tell your project owner as early as you can on the day. You do not need a note for the first week.

> Rest properly. Work done ill is usually done twice.
