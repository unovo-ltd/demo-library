# Welcome to Fernhill

![Fernhill Studio](images/fernhill.svg)

Fernhill is a design studio of eight people. We make brand identities, websites and printed matter for small organisations that care how they come across.

## How we work

- **One owner for every piece of work.** Whoever owns it decides, and asks when unsure.
- **Show work early.** A rough sketch on Tuesday beats a finished one on Friday.
- **Write it down.** If it matters next month, it belongs in this library.
